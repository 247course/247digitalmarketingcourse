## Introduction to Search Engine Marketing (SEM)

### What Is Search Engine Marketing?
Search Engine Marketing (SEM) is the strategy of increasing visibility in search engines through:
Organic rankings (SEO)
Paid advertising (PPC)
Data-driven optimization to drive qualified traffic
Think of search engines as the modern marketplace. SEM ensures your business appears at the exact moment someone is looking for your solution.

### How SEM Fits into the Marketing Funnel
Search touches every stage:
Top Funnel (Awareness): “What is email automation?”
Middle Funnel (Consideration): “Best email marketing tools 2026”
Bottom Funnel (Conversion): “Buy Mailchimp Pro plan”
Effective SEM maps keywords and ads to each stage.
 
## Understanding Search Engines & User Intent
### How Search Engines Work
Search engines like Google operate in three phases:
Crawling – Discover pages
Indexing – Store and categorize content
Ranking – Serve results based on relevance + authority

According to the Google Search Central guidelines, ranking depends on:
Content quality
Experience (UX)
Technical performance
Authority signals
User engagement
